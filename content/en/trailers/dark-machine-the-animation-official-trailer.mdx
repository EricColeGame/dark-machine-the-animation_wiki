## Story and World of Dark Machine The Animation

The premise is unusually specific for an esports series. DARK MACHINE is a globally popular competitive game that has reshaped Japan's economy, to the point where an exclusive economic zone called "Golden Valley" exists around it. Ajima, the seventh "Senki" — the title given to the game's strongest player — effectively controls the region through his dominance in the game.

The protagonist, Kairi, arrives in Golden Valley carrying his late grandfather's final words: "Find Senki." He crosses paths with a young girl named Rudora, who is being pursued by one of Ajima's subordinates. That encounter pulls Kairi into DARK MACHINE as a player, and his talent surfaces with each battle.

The central question the series poses is straightforward: can Kairi hold his own against Senki and carve out his own destiny?

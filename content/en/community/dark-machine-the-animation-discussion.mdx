## How to Join the Dark Machine The Animation Discussion Without Spoiling It

New episodes mean new viewers, and nothing kills a growing fandom faster than an untagged spoiler in a general thread. A few habits keep the conversation welcoming:

- **Tag every post with the episode number**, even when the spoiler feels obvious to you.
- **Give a new episode 48 hours** before posting untagged analysis in general channels.
- **Separate observation from theory** so readers know what's confirmed and what's speculation.
- **Link the timestamp** when you reference a specific shot — it makes replies far more useful.
- **Disagree with the take, not the person.** Anime fandoms are small; the people you argue with this week are the ones you'll be theorizing with next month.
